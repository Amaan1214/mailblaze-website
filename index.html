POLICIES

Transparent rules for responsible sending

Acceptable Use & Anti-Spam

MailBlaze services are intended for lawful, legitimate email. Unsolicited bulk email, purchased or harvested lists, phishing, impersonation, malware, deceptive messaging and provider-policy circumvention are prohibited.

Senders are responsible for maintaining appropriate consent records and complying with applicable laws and provider policies.

Privacy

Information provided to MailBlaze for support or business enquiries is used only for the purpose for which it was provided, legitimate service administration, security and compliance.

We do not sell personal information.

Unsubscribe & Suppression

Marketing recipients must have a clear way to opt out. Valid unsubscribe requests are honored promptly. Hard-bounced addresses and complaint-generating recipients are suppressed from subsequent marketing campaigns.

Terms of Service

Customers must use MailBlaze services lawfully and in accordance with applicable cloud-provider and email-service policies. We may refuse or discontinue assistance involving abusive, deceptive or unauthorized sending.
