Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

MailBlaze may collect information that visitors voluntarily submit, such as contact details or an email address used to request product updates. Information should be used for the purpose communicated at collection and protected using reasonable safeguards.

Marketing preferences

Marketing communication is intended for opted-in recipients. Recipients may request to unsubscribe, after which the address should be suppressed from future marketing communication.

Terms of Use

MailBlaze is intended for lawful, permission-based communication. Use for spam, phishing, deceptive messaging, scraped contacts or purchased mailing lists is prohibited.

Senders are responsible for maintaining appropriate consent, honoring recipient preferences and complying with applicable law and service-provider requirements.
